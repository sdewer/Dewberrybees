Nailsea Wildflower Honey

Local · Raw · Unpasteurised · Wildflower

£6.00 / 227g (8 oz) jar

100% Raw Unpasteurised Local to Nailsea Single Apiary Wildflower

Our bees forage across the fields and hedgerows in and around Nailsea. The honey they make is raw, unpasteurised and unblended. Nothing added, just lightly strained.

Because it's not heated, the honey keeps all its natural enzymes and pollen. The flavour changes a little from season to season depending on what's flowering — that's how it should be.

We now keep bees at two apiaries, Nailsea and Hewish. Each apiary's honey is extracted and jarred separately, never blended, so you know exactly where your honey comes from — right down to the postcode.

Raw honey crystallises over time. It's completely normal and actually a good sign. If yours sets solid, sit the jar in warm water for a bit and it'll loosen up.
