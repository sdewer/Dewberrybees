Hive Sponsorship

Support local beekeeping and help our bees thrive in Nailsea, North Somerset

By sponsoring one of our hives, you directly support the health of our local bee population, sustainable beekeeping, and the production of beautiful, natural honey. In return, sponsors receive regular updates from their hive, exclusive honey and a warm glow knowing they're doing something wonderful for nature.

Bee Buddy
(Individual Sponsorship)

Perfect for honey lovers and nature enthusiasts! As a Bee Buddy you'll adopt one of our bees in the apiary — your very own hardworking forager buzzing around Nailsea!

You'll receive 2 jars of delicious honey from the apiary each year, plus a personalised certificate to show off your bee credentials.

£25 per year

Pollination Partner
(Corporate Sponsorship)

A brilliant fit for forward-thinking companies, B-Corps and businesses with sustainability at their heart. As a Pollination Partner you'll sponsor a hive and receive naming rights to the colony's queen — yes, really!

You'll also enjoy brand recognition on our website, honey gifts for your team, and a certificate of sponsorship perfect for the office wall!

The Stewart Dewer Fund

This is where we support others: a portion of our profits every year will be donated to the Stewart Dewer Fund. Stewart is much loved and much missed by everyone who knew him. He was a huge advocate for young people and the small charity created in his name gives small grants to young people to help them in their studies, engage in training, and to support other life enriching and sporting opportunities — such as school camps, courses, clubs, and other activities.
